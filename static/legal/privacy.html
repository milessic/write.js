Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

This policy explains what data Write.JS ("the app", "we") stores, where it is stored and why. Write.JS is run by Miłosz Jura. Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to writejs.help@gmail.com.

1. Using Write.JS without an account

Without an account, your documents never leave your device. If you agree to storage in the consent dialog, documents and settings are saved in your browser's localStorage. We cannot see or recover them. Clearing your browser data, or using Clear localStorage in the menu, removes them for good.

2. Using Write.JS with an account

When you register, we store:

  • your username and email address,
  • your password, stored only as a salted hash (we never store or see the plain password),
  • when the account was created and when the password was last changed,
  • login sessions (access and refresh tokens, with their issue and expiry times),
  • failed login attempts, used to temporarily lock an account against password guessing,
  • password reset links you request (valid for a limited time).

When you sync, a copy of your notebook (documents and folder structure) is uploaded to our server and stored so you can open it on other devices. Your documents are not read, analysed, sold or used for advertising.

Your email address is used only to send messages you request, such as a password reset.

The login cookies are set only when you log in, because the account cannot work without them. Documents and settings are stored in localStorage only after you agree in the consent dialog. The two theme keys are written only when you pick a theme yourself, stay in your browser and are never sent to us or to the themes server. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising cookies.

4. Third parties

  • Google Fonts: the editor font is loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details as part of that request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Themes server: the look of the app may be loaded from the milessic-themes server, which receives the same request details.
  • Email: password reset emails are sent through an external SMTP provider.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

5. How long we keep data

Account data and synced notebooks are kept until you delete your account. Sessions, failed login records and password reset links expire automatically. Deleting your account removes your account and its related records from our database.

6. Your rights

You can access, export (Export Notebook), correct or delete your data at any time. You can delete your account from the Account menu, or ask us to do it by email. If you are in the EU/EEA, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions expire, and repeated failed logins lock the account temporarily. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your own backups of important documents.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
